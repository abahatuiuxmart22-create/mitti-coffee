Step 2 · the grind

Grind it last.

fifteen minutes, tops

Ground coffee gives up most of its aroma within fifteen minutes. We grind every cup to order, medium-fine, about the texture of table salt.

  • Dose18 g
  • Grind600–800 µm
  • Burrconical

Step 3 · the bloom

Let it bloom.

wait for the dome

A little hot water first, twice the coffee's weight. Fresh grounds release carbon dioxide and rise into a dome. Skip this and the water rushes past the flavour.

  • Water36 g
  • Temp93 °C
  • Wait0:45

Step 4 · the pour

Pour slow, in circles.

three minutes, worth it

The rest goes in three gentle pours through our clay dripper, keeping the water level steady. The last drop falls at around three minutes.

  • Ratio1 : 16
  • Water288 g
  • Time3:00

Step 5 · the cup

Make time taste better.

see you at the bar

Black, or over steamed milk with a heart on top. Our house coffee comes from smallholder farms in Ruma, Bandarban: natural process, medium roast, notes of jaggery, orange and cocoa.

A little about Mitti

More than coffee. A corner to come back to.

made with care

Mitti means soil in Bangla. It's where the coffee grows and what our drippers are made of. We buy straight from farms we've visited, roast on Tuesdays and Fridays, and brew every cup to order in Dhanmondi.
